FAQ

Is this completely free?
Yes. ThumbSnap is 100% free with no registration, no subscription, and no hidden charges.
What thumbnail sizes are available?
We offer Max Resolution (1280×720), High Quality (480×360), Medium Quality (320×180), and Standard (120×90) — all the sizes YouTube provides.
Can I use downloaded thumbnails?
Thumbnails belong to the video creator. You may use them for personal reference, research, or commentary. Always respect copyright and credit creators.
Does it work on mobile?
Yes. ThumbSnap works on all devices — iPhone, Android, tablet, and desktop browsers.
Why is Max Resolution sometimes not available?
YouTube only generates a Max Resolution thumbnail for videos that were uploaded in HD. If the Max Resolution is black or broken, use the HQ version instead — our tool falls back to it automatically.
